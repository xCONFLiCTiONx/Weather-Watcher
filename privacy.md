# Weather Watcher Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

Weather Watcher is an Android application that provides real-time meteorological data and alerts.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Location Data
To provide weather information for your current area, the app requires access to your location:
- **Precise and Approximate Location:** Used to fetch current weather data when the app is open.
- **Background Location:** If enabled, used to monitor weather conditions and provide alerts even when the app is not in the foreground.

### Device Information
We may check your battery optimization settings (`REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS`) to ensure that weather alerts are delivered reliably.

## 2. Third-Party Services
Weather Watcher retrieves data from the following third-party APIs:
- **National Weather Service (NWS):** For regional forecasts and alerts.
- **Open-Meteo:** For global weather data.
- **ArcGIS:** For location-based services.

When the app fetches weather data, your approximate latitude and longitude are sent to these services. These coordinates are used solely to retrieve the relevant weather information and are not associated with any personal identifiers by this application.

## 3. Data Storage and Privacy
- Your location data is processed in real-time and is not stored permanently on any server by us.
- We do not include any third-party advertising or trackers in this application.

## 4. User Control
You can manage or revoke location permissions at any time through your Android device's system settings.
